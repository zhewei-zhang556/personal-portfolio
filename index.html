RU Ready – Business Management Project

Team Project | Introduction to Management

Developed a student-focused moving service concept designed to provide affordable and reliable moving assistance for Rutgers students. As a team, we developed the business concept, customer value proposition, financial and strategic objectives, and plans for future growth.

The project also examined employee recruitment and motivation, operational processes, service effectiveness and efficiency, and process improvement. We used a SIPOC framework to evaluate the moving service process and proposed improvements to training, scheduling, equipment readiness, and customer service.
